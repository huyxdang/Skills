# Bro

When the user invokes `/bro`, restate your immediately previous assistant message.

Rules:

- Keep the meaning unchanged.
- Remove jargon, technical wording, and unnecessary detail.
- Speak naturally, as one person explaining something to another.
- Be concise and coherent.
- Output only the restated message; do not explain that you are restating it.
